Case study · Proptech

PermitVista

Instant building-permit history for any Florida property.

Live · paying customersFounder & sole engineer
Visit permitvista.com ↗
Scale
4M+ permits indexed · 50+ jurisdictions · results in seconds
Stack
Python · FastAPI · Azure SQL · Azure VM collector · Render · WeasyPrint · Square · SendGrid · GitHub Actions
Quality
CI pipeline · 71 automated tests

The problem

Open or expired permits derail closings. Agents, title companies, investors and homeowners used to check county portals one property at a time, each with its own search, its own formats and its own gaps. A single missed permit can hold up a sale or land on the new owner.

What I built

  • A FastAPI search engine merging a statewide permit index with 50+ county and city public-records integrations
  • Nightly bulk loads plus live lookups, with caching and de-duplication across sources
  • Automated open/expired flagging and branded PDF certificates ready for the closing file
  • Self-serve monetization: free teaser, per-permit or whole-address unlocks, and subscriptions, with Square payments and SendGrid email
  • CI with 71 automated tests, so every change to a jurisdiction integration is checked before it ships

Why it matters for your project

PermitVista is the same shape as most enterprise data work: dozens of inconsistent sources, a nightly bulk layer plus a live layer, de-duplication, and a product on top that people pay for. I built and operate every part of it.

Case study · Fintech

Espiral USA

A cross-border money app for US customers sending to Latin America, the UK and Europe.

Live · pre-launchCTO
Visit espiralusa.com ↗
Scale
5 corridors · 100 PRs and 135 tickets shipped in 8 weeks · two-person team
Stack
TypeScript · NestJS · Next.js 16 · Prisma · Postgres · Azure Container Apps · GitHub Actions · PostHog · Bridge · Stripe

The problem

Immigrants and small businesses pay 5–8% and wait days to move money abroad. Espiral gives US customers an account, same-day sends, and a way to get paid in dollars.

What I built

  • NestJS API over stablecoin payment rails (Bridge) with a webhook event store, so every external event is recorded and replayable
  • Nightly reconciliation between internal ledgers and the rail provider
  • Per-corridor kill switches and admin tooling for operational control
  • Stripe card funding, Mexico receive via CLABE, and payroll groundwork
  • Bilingual Next.js app (Spanish and English), deployed on Azure Container Apps with GitHub Actions CI and PostHog analytics

Why it matters for your project

Money movement is unforgiving. Event stores, reconciliation and kill switches are what let a two-person team operate a payments product safely, and they are what I bring to any integration where the records have to agree.

Case study · Trading systems

Polymarket Edge

An automated market-making and research platform for Polymarket US, running unattended with real capital.

Private build · in productionSole engineer
Scale
58 markets quoted continuously · ~200 fills/day · ~85 ms order latency · 25,000+ snapshots per game
Stack
Python · SQLite · WebSockets · Azure DevOps · systemd · Deribit & Odds APIs
Reconciliation
500+ fills matched to exchange records, $0.00 variance

The problem

Prediction-market prices lag real information, and small traders can't win on speed. The fee structure rewards resting orders instead. Polymarket Edge earns the maker/taker gap with model-driven quotes and treats risk control as the product.

What I built

  • A 24/7 quoting engine pricing 58 Bitcoin and politics markets from Deribit options data
  • Live order-book stream client with order routing in about 85 ms
  • A fill ledger reconciled fill-by-fill against exchange records: 500+ fills, $0.00 variance
  • Circuit breakers for drawdown, exposure and stale feeds, with hard stops that survive restarts and deploys
  • In-game NBA and MLB market recorders with replay-based strategy testing
  • CI/CD to a cloud VM, deployed on every push and supervised by systemd

Why it matters for your project

This is the system I'd point to if you asked whether I can be trusted with production money. It runs around the clock, reconciles to the penny, and stops itself before a person has to.

Want one for your own account?

I build the same kind of system for traders and small funds under the Trading Systems Build engagement: your strategy and your account, with the execution, reconciliation and risk layer proven here. You keep the keys; I never manage money or sell signals.

Moviz

A peer-to-peer car rental marketplace for Guadalajara, with optional chauffeurs.

Live betaCo-founder & CTO
Visit Moviz ↗
Scale
~22,700 lines of code · 63 API endpoints · 129 automated tests · v2.8.0
Stack
Next.js · TypeScript · FastAPI · Azure SQL · Azure Blob · Entra External ID · Render · GitHub Actions

The problem

Guadalajara car owners have no local Turo-style marketplace, and renters need Spanish-first booking with deposits and optional drivers. Moviz is that marketplace, built for how rentals actually work in Mexico.

What I built

  • Listings and rule-based booking: hourly rentals, deposits, cancellations, late fees and odometer check-in
  • Travel zones with map pins; optional chauffeur and aircraft quote listings
  • Google and Apple sign-in on Microsoft Entra External ID; integrated payments
  • In-app chat with contact-detail blocking to keep transactions on-platform
  • Bilingual UI, CI, release versioning and a 129-test suite via GitHub Actions

Why it matters for your project

A two-sided marketplace is identity, payments, rules and trust all at once. Moviz shows the full build from auth to release process, shipped by a small team at startup pace.

JuryFlow

A multi-firm legal CRM, billing and client-intake platform.

In development · beta plannedCo-founder & engineer
Stack
Python · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · SQLAlchemy · React · TypeScript · Docker · pytest
Integrations
Clio · Filevine · Cerenade · LawPay · Affirm · Google Workspace

The problem

Law firms lose leads and fees across disconnected tools and manual follow-up. JuryFlow brings intake, case tracking and billing into one platform for firm owners, intake staff and billing teams.

What I'm building

  • Multi-tenant FastAPI backend with per-firm data isolation, JWT auth and role-based access
  • React dashboard: lead pipeline, case timelines and CFO analytics
  • Automated payment reminders by email and SMS; online payments via LawPay and Affirm

Why it matters for your project

Multi-tenant isolation and two-way sync with incumbent systems are the hard parts of any vertical SaaS. JuryFlow is that pattern applied to legal; the same architecture fits any industry with entrenched tools.
